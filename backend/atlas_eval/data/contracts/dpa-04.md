DATA PROTECTION ADDENDUM

Fleetmind SAS (the Controller) and Routewise Ltd (the Processor)

1. Purpose
The Processor processes the location data and names of the Controller's drivers in order to plan
delivery routes, for the duration of the routing services.

2. Processing on instructions
The Processor shall process the personal data only on documented instructions from the
Controller.

3. Staff
The Processor shall ensure that its staff who process the personal data have committed
themselves to confidentiality.

4. Protection of the data
The Processor shall use reasonable efforts to keep the personal data safe.

5. Data subjects
The Processor shall assist the Controller in responding to requests from data subjects to
exercise their rights.

6. Breaches and impact assessments
The Processor shall assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with the obligations pursuant
to Articles 32 to 36 of the GDPR.

7. Return of data
On termination, the Processor shall delete or return all personal data, at the choice of the
Controller.

8. Information
The Processor shall answer the Controller's reasonable written questions about its processing.
