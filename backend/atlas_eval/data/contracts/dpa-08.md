CUSTOMER DATA TERMS

These terms apply where Vantage Cloud Inc. (the Supplier) handles Customer Personal Data on
behalf of the Customer.

1. Processing details
The Supplier handles the Customer's end users' names, account identifiers and usage logs to run
the Vantage analytics platform, for the subscription term. Schedule 1 describes the processing
further.

2. Customer directions
The Supplier will act solely upon the Customer's written directions when handling Customer
Personal Data, unless the law it is subject to requires otherwise.

3. Personnel
All Supplier personnel with access to Customer Personal Data are subject to a contractual or
statutory duty of secrecy.

4. Safeguards
The Supplier maintains the safeguards described in Schedule 2, which meet the requirements of
Article 32 GDPR.

5. Subcontractors
The Supplier may appoint subcontractors to handle Customer Personal Data with the Customer's
prior specific consent in writing.

6. Cooperation
The Supplier will help the Customer answer individuals' requests to exercise their data
protection rights, and will help the Customer meet its obligations under Articles 32 to 36
GDPR, including breach notification.

7. Exit
On expiry of the subscription the Supplier will, as the Customer elects, either hand back or
erase all Customer Personal Data.

8. Verification
The Supplier will provide the Customer with all information needed to show that these terms are
complied with, and will permit and contribute to audits and inspections by the Customer or its
auditor.
