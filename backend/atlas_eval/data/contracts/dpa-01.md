DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

between Northwind Retail Ltd (the Controller) and Cloudline Support SA (the Processor)

This agreement forms part of the support services agreement between the parties (the Services
Agreement).

1. Subject matter and duration
The Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller to operate the Controller's
customer support desk, for the duration of the Services Agreement.

1.1 The personal data processed are the names, e-mail addresses, telephone numbers and support
messages of the Controller's customers, who are the data subjects. The nature and purpose of
the processing are the storage, routing and answering of support requests.

1.2 The Controller determines the purposes and means of the processing and may give
instructions to the Processor at any time.

2. Instructions
The Processor shall process the personal data only on documented instructions from the
Controller, including with regard to transfers of personal data to a third country, unless
required to do so by Union or Member State law.

3. Confidentiality
The Processor shall ensure that persons authorised to process the personal data have committed
themselves to confidentiality.

4. Security
The Processor shall implement the technical and organisational measures required pursuant to
Article 32 of the GDPR, as described in Annex 2.

5. Sub-processors
The Processor may engage other processors with the general written authorisation of the
Controller. The Processor shall impose on any other processor the same data protection
obligations as set out in this agreement.

6. Requests from data subjects
Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Processor shall assist the Controller by
appropriate technical and organisational measures in responding to requests from data subjects
to exercise their rights.

7. End of the services
Upon termination of the Services Agreement, the Processor shall delete all personal data
processed on behalf of the Controller within 30 days.

8. Liability
Each party's liability under this agreement is limited as set out in the Services Agreement.

9. Governing law
This agreement is governed by the law of Ireland.
