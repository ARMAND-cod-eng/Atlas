PROCESSING TERMS

Brightpath Learning BV (the Controller) appoints Tutorbase Ltd (the Processor).

1. Scope
The Processor hosts the Controller's online courses and processes the names, e-mail addresses
and course results of the Controller's students for that purpose, for the term of the hosting
agreement.

2. Security
The Processor shall implement the technical and organisational measures required by Article 32
of the GDPR.

3. Sub-processors
The Processor shall not engage another processor without the prior written authorisation of the
Controller, and shall impose the same data protection obligations on any processor it engages.

4. Requests and assistance
The Processor shall assist the Controller in responding to requests from data subjects, and in
ensuring compliance with the obligations under Articles 32 to 36 of the GDPR.

5. Termination
At the end of the services, the Processor shall, at the choice of the Controller, delete or
return all personal data.

6. Information
The Processor shall make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate
compliance, and shall allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections.
