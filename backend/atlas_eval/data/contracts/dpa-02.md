DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

between Harbour Insurance plc (the Controller) and Ledgerworks GmbH (the Processor)

1. Details of the processing
1.1 The Processor processes personal data to administer the Controller's insurance claims, for as
long as the claims administration agreement is in force.
1.2 The personal data are the names, policy numbers, contact details and claim histories of the
Controller's policyholders. The nature and purpose of the processing are the recording,
assessment and payment of claims.
1.3 The Controller's obligations and rights are those set out in this agreement and in Article 24
of the GDPR.

2. Instructions
The Processor shall process the personal data only on documented instructions from the
Controller, including with regard to transfers to a third country or an international
organisation.

3. Confidentiality
The Processor shall ensure that every person authorised to process the personal data has
committed themselves to confidentiality or is under an appropriate statutory obligation of
confidentiality.

4. Security
The Processor shall take all measures required pursuant to Article 32 of the GDPR.

5. Other processors
5.1 The Processor shall not engage another processor without the prior specific written
authorisation of the Controller.
5.2 Where the Processor engages another processor, it shall impose on that processor the same
data protection obligations as are set out in this agreement, by contract.

6. Data subjects' rights
The Processor shall assist the Controller by appropriate technical and organisational measures,
insofar as this is possible, in fulfilling its obligation to respond to requests for exercising
the data subject's rights.

7. Assistance
The Processor shall assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with the obligations pursuant
to Articles 32 to 36 of the GDPR, taking into account the nature of the processing and the
information available to the Processor.

8. End of the processing
At the choice of the Controller, the Processor shall delete or return all the personal data to
the Controller after the end of the provision of services, and delete existing copies.

9. Audits
The Processor shall make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate
compliance with this agreement, and shall allow for and contribute to audits, including
inspections, conducted by the Controller or another auditor mandated by the Controller.
